# Changelog

Pitlane HQ is in **beta**. Pitlane HQ Agent (the PC app), the web app and the phone apps share one version
number, `0.MINOR.PATCH`. A MINOR version brings a set of new features, and fixes go in as "Fixes and
improvements". 1.0.0 comes when the beta ends. Newest first.

## 0.5.0 beta

- The coach learns from what happens after its tips, puts the ones that help most first, lets you rate them with 👍
  or 👎 and remembers your habits across tracks.
- Under each tip you see where the fast drivers brake and how fast they take the apex.
- New throttle tips for when you open it too fast or take too long to reach full throttle.
- Each lap's line comes from the game's real position, and the corners are numbered by the track's shape.
- The Coach overlay works live and tells you about the next corner while you drive, with four styles to pick before
  you go out (card, column, corner drawn or big for VR), and it says "Next turn (T7)" or however you prefer.
- Overlays can no longer be made smaller than their normal size.
- Telemetry has a Basic and a Full mode with the speed, throttle, brake, delta and gear traces, and Compare laps live
  against your best, your last lap, the next level or the record.
- Standings show the gap to the leader or the interval, a class filter when several classes race and your position lap
  by lap, and Tyres show their wear.
- Rev beep is simpler, pick the car, how it beeps (iRacing lights, your laps or your own RPM, one for every gear or
  each gear) and its pitch and volume.
- Overlays are one list where you open each one now or when you get in the car, with the presets and its settings at
  hand.
- Leagues are open to everyone, and the PC app no longer zooms with Ctrl and the mouse wheel.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.7 beta

- Home is new. You see your last race with your position lap by lap, your recent sessions tagged as Race,
  Qualifying, Practice or vs AI, the iRating and licence of each discipline and the days you drove.
- The leagues list now also shows the most active leagues on Discord and Reddit.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.6 beta

- Compare in Community now opens on the same track, and only if you have a valid lap there. A lap from another
  track never shows up as lap B.
- In Recent records the list scrolls with the page and the record you pick stays in view.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.5 beta

- Community is all new. Summary shows your leaderboards, how much your best lap has come down and the latest
  records. Leaderboards has the sector times and a map that shows in which sectors each driver is faster or slower
  than you. Recent records shows the latest ones.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.4 beta

- A lap is only invalid if you leave the track or lose the connection, just like in iRacing. A Loss of Control or
  a Car Contact only counts when it puts you off the track, and going through the pit lane no longer ruins a lap.
- Incidents now have the same names as in iRacing (Off Track, Loss of Control and Car Contact).
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.3 beta

- The phone app looks new. Home starts with your last race, you can run your finger along your iRating and the
  coach tells you first how far you are and what to work on.
- An offline race against the AI no longer shows up as your licence.
- Laps cut short by a reset or a tow to the pits never count as a best lap or a record. A lap through the pit lane
  still counts.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.2 beta

- The same car under different badges now shares one leaderboard, record and coach. That goes for the Super
  Formula SF23's Honda and Toyota, NASCAR's Chevrolet, Ford and Toyota and the Supercars.
- You can read and write your track notes from the phone, on each track's page.
- Driver profiles show their leaderboards, records and top 3 finishes at a glance, and on the phone every recent
  record opens its driver's profile.
- You can no longer shrink an overlay until it's unreadable.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.1 beta

- Pitlane HQ Agent records every lap you drive in iRacing with nothing to set up. Your laps go to your account and
  you can open them on the PC, the web or your phone.
- Compare any two laps with speed, inputs, time gap, map, sectors, braking points and racing line. The coach shows
  you the corners where you lose the most against real faster laps, the record and the driver just ahead of your
  pace.
- Overlays over the game with the relative, standings, radar, delta bar, fuel, tyres, map, flags, braking markers
  and more. They come with presets and Auto, and they work in VR through SteamVR.
- Leaderboards and records by car and track for every discipline, plus a page for each track.
- A summary after every race, your iRating and licence in every discipline and your own notes on the drivers you
  race with.
- A rev beep tells you when to shift up at the revs you pick for each car. You'll find it in Telemetry → Rev beep.
- Follow your race live on your phone or in a browser, or share a code so a friend can watch.
- One app for Windows that updates itself, the web at pitlanehq.app and the Android app, in English, Spanish and
  Portuguese. There's also the Pitlane HQ bot on Discord.
