# Changelog

Pitlane HQ is in **beta**. Pitlane HQ Agent (the PC app), the web app and the phone apps share one version
number: `0.MINOR.PATCH`. A MINOR version is a set of new features; fixes ride along as "Fixes and
improvements". 1.0.0 comes when the beta ends. Newest first.

## 0.4.2 beta

- **One leaderboard for one car**: cars that are the same under different badges (the Super Formula SF23's Honda
  and Toyota, NASCAR's Chevrolet, Ford and Toyota, the Supercars) share one leaderboard, one record and one coach.
- **Track notes on the phone**: your notes on each track, on the track page of the phone apps, and you can write
  them there too.
- **Driver profiles**: leaderboards, records and top 3 places at a glance, and every recent record opens its
  driver's profile on the phone.
- **Overlays** can no longer be made too small to read.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.1 beta

- **Telemetry from every lap**: Pitlane HQ Agent records every lap you drive in iRacing, with nothing to set up, and
  the laps go to your account: open them on the PC, on the web or on your phone.
- **Lap analysis and a coach**: compare any two laps (speed, inputs, time gap, the map, the sectors, the braking
  points and the racing line), and a coach that says which corners cost the most against real faster laps: the
  record and the driver just ahead of your pace.
- **Overlays on top of the game**: relative, standings, radar, delta bar, fuel, tyres, track map, flags, braking
  markers and more, drawn by Pitlane HQ; presets, Auto, and the overlays in VR through SteamVR.
- **Leaderboards and records** by car and track for every discipline, recent records and each track's page.
- **Race summaries** after every race, your iRating and licence of every discipline, and driver notes on the
  drivers you meet.
- **Rev beep**: a beep to change up at the revs you choose, for each car, in Telemetry → Rev beep.
- **Telemetry on your phone**: follow your race live on the phone or a browser, and share a code so a friend can
  watch.
- **One app for Windows** that updates itself, the web at pitlanehq.app and the Android app, in English, Spanish and
  Portuguese, and the Pitlane HQ bot on Discord.
